Approve a next strategic block focused on converting the validated architecture into observed owner-local intelligence value while expanding only the highest-priority measured source gaps.

`Phase 22 — Operational Evidence Pilot & High-Priority Coverage Expansion`

This proposal was explicitly approved by the owner on 2026-09-19 and is superseded as the active authorization record by `docs/decisions/PHASE_22_OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_HIGH_PRIORITY_COVERAGE_ROADMAP_DECISION_2026-09-19.md`.

## Why this block

Phase 21 closed the coverage-policy and controlled-onboarding framework but left:

- 20 required cells `MISSING_EXPECTED_COVERAGE`;
- 5 required cells `THIN`;
- 1 required cell `DEGRADED_COLLECTION`;
- only 1 required cell structurally `ADEQUATE`;
- post-Wave-A verification/contradiction/forecast-input effects `NOT_OBSERVED`.

Meanwhile Phase 14, 15, 16 and 19 already provide sufficient owner-local readiness to run a bounded evidence-producing pilot without public/shared activation.

## Proposed strategic objective

Generate real owner-local semantic and operator evidence while reducing the highest-priority required source gaps.

The block must couple source expansion to downstream evidence:

`DISCOVER -> QUALIFY -> ONBOARD -> MEASURE HEALTH/PROVENANCE -> COLLECT -> VERIFY -> ANALYZE -> FORECAST INPUT -> OWNER FEEDBACK`

## Proposed sequence

### P22.0 — Entry Convergence & Owner Gates

Confirm canonical state, preserve Phase 21 closure, and record explicit owner decisions for any operational activation and source-wave onboarding.

No mutation before the relevant owner gate.

### P22.1 — Bounded Owner-Only Operational Pilot

Use validated Phase 14/19 capabilities in owner-local mode to accumulate a timestamped, provenance-bound operational corpus.

No public ingress, shared runtime or production/live declaration.

### P22.2 — Wave-B Candidate Discovery & Qualification

Use the P21.4 `B_HIGH_REQUIRED` cohort as the default planning basis.

Public/free/anonymous-first. No paid-source selection by implication.

### P22.3 — Controlled High-Priority Onboarding

Onboard approved candidates in small reversible cohorts through the P20.5 contract.

Every cohort requires:

- fixtures/tests before activation;
- provenance classification;
- health/freshness measurement;
- rollback/disable semantics;
- no automatic independence credit.

### P22.4 — Operational Coverage Rebaseline

Recompute deterministic coverage after each accepted cohort.

Track required-cell status deltas rather than raw source-count success.

### P22.5 — Semantic Corpus & Verification Observation

Measure real post-expansion semantic corpus behavior through P13.5/P13.6.

Observe, do not assume:

- verification decision distribution;
- unresolved claims;
- provenance completeness;
- evidence independence;
- source contribution to verified/partly verified/unresolved outcomes.

### P22.6 — Contradiction / Analysis / Forecast-Input Impact

Measure downstream effects on:

- contradiction detection and unresolved workload;
- analytical coverage;
- forecast-input evidence breadth;
- data freshness/coverage limitations.

No forecast or coverage metric may promote factual truth.

### P22.7 — Owner Utility & Quality Observation

Use the Phase-16 operator/feedback model to observe:

- usefulness;
- timeliness;
- noise;
- correction requests;
- delivery/read-model utility.

Feedback remains advisory and cannot rewrite factual verification automatically.

### P22.8 — Phase Acceptance

Proposed final gate:

`PHASE_22_OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_HIGH_PRIORITY_COVERAGE_VALIDATED`

Acceptance should require observed evidence, not architecture-only readiness.

## Proposed non-authorization

Approval of the strategic block should still **not** by itself authorize:

- paid providers;
- shared runtime;
- public ingress;
- migration `033`;
- production/live cutover;
- Plugin build/publication;
- external publication;
- canonical storage cutover.

Operational activation and source-wave onboarding should remain explicitly bounded owner decisions.
