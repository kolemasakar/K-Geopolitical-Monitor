## Proposal

Adopt the next strategic direction as an **evidence-yield dual track**: selectively improve required source coverage while explicitly increasing underlying-origin/corroboration depth and producing a bounded owner-facing evidence sample.

Working title only:

`Phase 23 — Evidence Depth, Corroboration & Operational Yield`

This document does **not** create or authorize Phase 23.

## Why this direction

Phase 22 closed with measurable but narrow coverage improvement:

- required missing cells: `20 -> 18`;
- adequate required cells: unchanged at `1`;
- healthy/fresh-path delta: `0`;
- 28 canonical claims observed, all `DETECTED / ATTRIBUTION_ONLY`;
- underlying origin unresolved for all 28;
- downstream intelligence uplift: not observed;
- owner utility uplift: not observed.

Therefore the next block should optimize source work for downstream evidence value rather than raw source count.

## Proposed objectives

A future block should require measurable movement in at least three dimensions:

### Coverage

- reduce required `MISSING_EXPECTED_COVERAGE` and/or `THIN` cells;
- preserve explicit health/freshness measurement;
- repair or replace blocked acquisition paths without silently relaxing bounded resource controls.

### Evidence depth

- resolve underlying-origin metadata where evidence permits;
- populate explicit evidence relations and independence assessments;
- obtain a cohort capable of exercising support/contradiction policy without count-only promotion;
- preserve P13.5/P13.6 as sole factual-verification authority.

### Operational yield

- measure whether the resulting evidence contributes to contradiction handling, analytical coverage, and forecast inputs;
- produce a bounded persisted owner-facing delivery sample;
- record explicit usefulness/timeliness/noise/correction feedback rather than infer it.

## Candidate work sequence

Proposal only:

1. `P23.0` — entry convergence and explicit owner gates.
2. `P23.1` — B1 blocker remediation + selective source readiness.
3. `P23.2` — underlying-origin/provenance resolution cohort.
4. `P23.3` — corroboration/evidence-relation population.
5. `P23.4` — selective evidence-yield source expansion.
6. `P23.5` — downstream intelligence-yield observation.
7. `P23.6` — bounded owner-facing delivery/feedback observation.
8. `P23.7` — acceptance.

## Proposed success rule

Do not accept the future block on source-count growth alone.

A candidate source or repair should have an explicit expected contribution to one or more of:

```text
REQUIRED_COVERAGE
PROVENANCE_ORIGIN_DEPTH
CORROBORATION_OR_CONTRADICTION
ANALYTICAL_OR_FORECAST_INPUT
OWNER_UTILITY_EVIDENCE
```

The final phase decision should require measured evidence, including explicit fail-closed results where uplift is absent.
