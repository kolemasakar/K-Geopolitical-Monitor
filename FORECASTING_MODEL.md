# FORECASTING_MODEL

Defines forecasting principles for K-Geopolitical Monitor.

Version: 1.0
Status: APPROVED

## Forecast Principle

Forecasts are scenario-based assessments derived from verified information, relationships, trends, actor behaviour and constraints.

Forecasts are not facts.

## Horizons

- SHORT-TERM: up to 1 month
- MEDIUM-TERM: up to 2 years
- LONG-TERM: up to 20 years
- GLOBAL_EVOLUTIONARY: over 20 years

## Scenario Structure

Each significant scenario should include:

- probability or relative likelihood;
- confidence;
- drivers;
- constraints;
- triggers;
- inhibitors;
- invalidation signals.

## Forecast Lifecycle

Forecasts are versioned:

Forecast -> New Evidence -> Update -> Outcome Evaluation -> Calibration
